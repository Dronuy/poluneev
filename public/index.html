What we do

One radiologist's read — drafted by AI, signed by a human.

A modern MSK radiology read is a chain of repeatable measurements and a paragraph of clinical interpretation. The repeatable measurements take minutes; the paragraph is the part that benefits from a human.

Poluneev automates the measurements end-to-end and emits a structured, literature-anchored output. The radiologist stays in the loop where it matters — the wording, the caveats, the signature.

Every threshold, every cutoff, every category in our output is tied to a published reference. Indices are computed deterministically from DICOM voxels; no scores are ever produced without a clear numeric chain back to the source measurement.

The first module — the PFI Engine — closes the loop on patellofemoral instability MRI: 14 indices across 4 clinical domains, validated on 530 fastMRI knee studies, ready for review.

What the PFI Engine does

Drop a DICOM study. Get a clinical-ready report in seconds.

⚡

Fully automated

From DICOM upload to clinical output in seconds. No manual landmarking, no slice picking, no measurement loop. The pipeline handles series identification, slice selection, landmark detection, and threshold application end-to-end.

☷

Multi-domain

14 quantitative indices across 4 clinical domains: patellar height (IS, CDI, PTI), trochlear morphology (3 cartilage + 4 bone indices), lateralization (PT-TG cart), and patellar tilt.

📚

Literature-based

Every threshold from a peer-reviewed reference: Insall 1971, Caton 1982, Biedert 2006, Tanaka 2023, Pfirrmann 2000, Carrillon 2000, Sallay 1996, Hinckel 2017. No black-box scores.
